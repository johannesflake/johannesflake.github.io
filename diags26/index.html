S4A2 Graduate Seminar Representation Theory:
Diagrammatic Methods in Representation Theory
-----------------------

by Jonas Antor[*] and Johannes Flake[*]


* Dates

Wednesdays, 2-4 pm, SemR 0.008, Endenicher Allee 60

* Topics

- Temperley-Lieb algebras and SL2
- Brauer algebras and On
- Partition algebra and Sn
- Walled Brauer algebras and GLn 
- Hecke algebras and Birman-Murakami-Wenzl algebras
- Category of tangles and Reshetikhin-Turaev invariants
- Kuperberg's spider categories
- Web categories for SLn 
- Cobordism categories and TQFTs
- Khovanov's Heisenberg category
- Khovanov link homology
